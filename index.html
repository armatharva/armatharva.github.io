A tiny look at study time

This beginner-friendly analysis groups a small, fictional study-hours dataset by topic. The sample is synthetic—not a record of anyone’s actual study habits—and makes a safe, simple way to practice reading and summarizing CSV data.

Tools: Python’s CSV and statistics modules.

What I practiced: Reading tabular data, grouping observations, calculating averages, and labeling sample data honestly.
